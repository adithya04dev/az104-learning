# Mission: Become an Azure Administrator and pass AZ-104

## Why
Build administrator-level Azure skill that is useful at work, not just memorized exam knowledge, and use that skill to pass Microsoft Exam AZ-104.

## Success looks like
- Explain why an Azure administration choice is correct and why plausible alternatives are wrong.
- Complete representative administration tasks in the Azure portal and with Azure CLI or PowerShell.
- Read and make small changes to ARM templates or Bicep files.
- Troubleshoot identity, storage, compute, networking, monitoring, and recovery scenarios.
- Consistently meet the agreed readiness threshold on timed, mixed-domain practice assessments.
- Pass Exam AZ-104.

## Constraints
- The learner passed AZ-900 last year and has basic Azure/cloud exposure at work.
- Identity and governance have received a conceptual first pass, but hands-on and scenario proficiency are not yet established.
- Prefer concise, structured explanations that still develop depth.
- Study time, target exam date, Azure lab access, and lab budget are still to be confirmed.

## Out of scope
- Deep solution-architecture, application-development, and specialist security topics not required by the current AZ-104 outline.
- Memorizing question dumps or relying on practice scores without hands-on competence.
