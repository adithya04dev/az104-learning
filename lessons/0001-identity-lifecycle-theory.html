Identity lifecycle · Product scenario

Give the right person the right identity.

Northstar Retail is launching Pulse, a loyalty product. Employees, a design agency, and support contractors all need different forms of identity. Your skill is to model who they are before deciding what they may access.

≈18 minutesAZ-104: Entra users & groups3 scenario checks

The lifecycle is a chain, not a checkbox

1 · RepresentCreate or invite the correct user type.
2 · OrganizeUse groups for scalable membership.
3 · EnableAssign required licenses and recovery capability.
4 · RetireBlock, remove access, then delete under policy.

Microsoft Entra ID establishes the identity. It does not automatically grant rights to Azure resources. That authorization decision belongs to Azure RBAC and appears in the next theory page.

Choose the identity type from the relationship

Internal member

Typical employee. Authentication and account lifecycle are managed in your tenant. This is the default mental model for staff working on Pulse.

External guest

A partner or contractor who authenticates with an external identity and receives guest-level privileges in your tenant. Use this for the design agency instead of inventing permanent employee identities.

Member/guest is not the same as internal/external

The current Entra model distinguishes where authentication happens from the user's access posture. AZ-104 questions may give you enough clues to distinguish a normal employee from a B2B guest even when they do not use this exact vocabulary.

Decision rule: identify the person's organizational relationship and authentication home first. Permissions come later.

Groups convert people management into role management

Instead of assigning the same entitlement to twelve engineers, create a security group such as pulse-api-operators, manage membership, and grant access to the group. Onboarding and offboarding then become membership changes.

Realistic product decision

A developer changes teams

Meera leaves Pulse for the payments platform. Direct role assignments require hunting for every entitlement. Group-based access lets the administrator remove her from the Pulse group and add her to the payments group.

Operational benefitFewer assignments and a clearer review boundary.
Exam signalMany users need the same access → prefer a group.

Licensing enables a service; it does not authorize a resource

A license can enable features for a user, while a role assignment authorizes an action. Do not answer an access question with “assign a license” unless the missing capability is actually license-gated.

SSPR password reset requires appropriate licensing for users who benefit from it. Microsoft’s current guided tutorial uses Entra ID P1, an Authentication Policy Administrator, a non-admin test user, and a test group. Always verify your tenant SKU before planning the lab.

SSPR is a controlled recovery workflow

SSPR can be enabled for selected users or all users. Users must register allowed authentication methods before they can prove identity during recovery. A sensible rollout starts with a pilot group, measures failures, then broadens scope.

Incident at Pulse

Monday morning password surge

After a long weekend, support receives 80 reset requests. The durable fix is not to grant more help-desk administrators. Pilot SSPR for a controlled group, require suitable verification methods, keep registration data current, and monitor the experience.

Check your decisions

1. An external design agency needs access to one project for three months. What identity approach best matches the relationship?

2. Twenty support engineers need the same Azure access. What should you manage first?

3. SSPR is enabled for a pilot group, but a user has not registered an allowed authentication method. What is the core problem?
