Do not reveal quiz answers early. In labs, write your prediction before each change, capture evidence after it, and explain any mismatch. If a permission or license blocks you, use the observation path and record the exact blocker.

Fictional company and product names are used so the scenarios feel operational without implying facts about a real organization. Ask your Codex teacher whenever a result differs from your prediction.
