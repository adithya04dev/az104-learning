# Teaching notes

- The learner wants administrator-level depth and exam readiness, not an AZ-900-style overview.
- Avoid reteaching basic identity/governance definitions unless a diagnostic exposes a gap.
- Use short, tightly scoped lessons followed immediately by a scenario, prediction, or hands-on task.
- Require the learner to explain reasoning, especially least privilege, scope, inheritance, redundancy, routing, availability, monitoring, and recovery choices.
- Track conceptual understanding, procedural ability, and troubleshooting ability separately.
- Unknowns to settle: target exam date, weekly study time, preferred portal/CLI/PowerShell balance, lab access, and acceptable Azure spend.
- The learner wants reusable theory pages paired with practical labs and realistic product scenarios.
